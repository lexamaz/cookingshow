Cooking Show Classroom

The CS Classroom is where you learn more about yourself as a cook and as a storyteller. Each unit takes one concept card and one brief through two lessons. Moving from novice to intermediate, you try to level-up the same dish over time. Because nobody's scoring you, it's a space for reflection and growth. When you're ready, head to the CS Kitchen to take a unit test, which helps you to prepare for the CS Competition that lies ahead.

You're not just teaching yourself to cook, you're learning how to tell your story. Because each lesson is based on a concept, the video lessons provide models of dishes that meet the brief and narratives inspired by the concept(s).

Classroom · Practice Formative assessment: informs the learning process · immediate, actionable feedback · low stakes · active and ongoing
Kitchen · Performance Summative assessment: evaluates the learning progress · levels of achievement · higher stakes · end of unit
